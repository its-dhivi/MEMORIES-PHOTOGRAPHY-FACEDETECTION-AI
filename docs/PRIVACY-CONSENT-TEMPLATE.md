# Privacy notice and biometric search consent draft

**Draft for qualified Indian privacy counsel. Do not publish this template unchanged or treat it as a compliance determination.** Confirm the law and rules in force on the launch date, the parties' roles, child-specific requirements, notice language, retention and complaint process.

## Event photography and photo finder notice

**Who operates this service:** [photographer/business legal name and contact]. **Event:** [event name/date]. **Privacy contact:** [name/email/phone/address].

The event organizer/photographer has provided photographs to create and operate this event gallery. The gallery may include images of guests other than you. Access is limited to this event and is available until [date]. Photographs are stored on [describe self-hosted location/operator and any subprocessors]. We do not use this event gallery to identify people across events.

If you choose face search, the service processes the selfie you submit and face representations derived from it and event photographs to find likely photographs in this event. Search is optional. You can browse the gallery without face search where enabled. The service may return incorrect photographs or miss photographs; verify each result before downloading or sharing. Search results and the selfie are subject to the retention periods shown below.

We use the submitted selfie and related biometric representations only for this event-specific photo retrieval purpose. We do not use them for identity verification, access control, advertising, or unrelated model training. [Confirm whether this statement matches actual operation before publication.]

**Retention:** selfie: [period and deletion trigger]; derived query representation: [period]; event face representations: [period/event deletion rule]; original photographs: [period/event deletion rule]; search results and access logs: [period]. **Withdrawal/deletion:** contact [privacy contact] or use [in-product method]. Explain any short delay and any legally required retention. Already downloaded copies cannot be recalled.

You may decline, withdraw consent, request information/correction/deletion as applicable, or contact [privacy contact]. Withdrawal stops future processing and revokes active searches as described here; it cannot remove copies already downloaded by you or other recipients. For concerns not resolved by the operator, provide [applicable escalation route after counsel review].

## Optional explicit face-search consent

I am choosing to submit a selfie to search for photographs of me in **[event name] only**. I have read the notice above, including that this is optional, may be inaccurate, and has the stated retention/deletion periods. I consent to processing my selfie and derived face representation for event-scoped photo retrieval. I understand how to withdraw consent or request deletion through [method/contact].

☐ I consent to event-scoped face search. ☐ I do not consent.

If you are not legally able to provide consent for yourself, do not submit another person's selfie. Provide a separate guardian-consent process only after counsel has approved it.

## Photographer / organizer permission record (draft)

Record the organizer's authority/permission for collection, gallery publication, and face-search use as separate decisions; do not assume permission to photograph automatically permits biometric search. Record scope, date, event, permitted uses, notice version, withdrawal contact, and any restrictions. Avoid representing this record as consent by every photographed guest.

## Counsel review checklist

- Identify each party's role and lawful basis for each processing operation.
- Review the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and DPDP Rules, 2025 commencement/transition timeline as applicable to the actual launch date; confirm any other sectoral, contract, child-safety, consumer, and copyright obligations.
- Confirm meaningful, itemized, accessible notice and consent, including local-language versions if needed.
- Set actual processor/subprocessor, hosting, access, encryption, retention, breach handling, grievance, deletion, withdrawal, and backup-expiry terms.
- Decide whether face embeddings or other derived artifacts require special handling under applicable law or contract, independent of any statutory label.
- Review event photography rights, minors, group-photo nonparticipants, export/downloads, and sharing with guests.
- Confirm audit log content and ensure selfies, embeddings, raw tokens, and signed URLs are not logged.

Sources for counsel to verify: [DPDP Act, 2023 (Official Gazette)](https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2023/248045.pdf) and [DPDP Rules, 2025 and commencement notifications (MeitY)](https://www.meity.gov.in/DPDP).
