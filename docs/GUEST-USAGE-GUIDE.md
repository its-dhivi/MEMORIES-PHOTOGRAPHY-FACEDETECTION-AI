# Guest photo-finding guide

Use the event link or QR code supplied by the photographer. The event may ask for a PIN before you continue.

## Search for your photos

1. Read the event privacy notice. Face matching is optional and requires your explicit consent. You can leave without searching.
2. If you agree, allow camera access and capture a clear, front-facing selfie with one unobstructed face. Use even light, look toward the camera, and avoid strong backlight. If camera access is unavailable, upload a portrait instead.
3. Wait for search to finish. Results are limited to the event and session. Face matching can miss photos or show an incorrect match, so review results before downloading.
4. Open a photo to preview it, select the photos you want, and use individual or bulk download if the photographer enabled it. Downloads are private and time-limited.
5. Use the event privacy page to withdraw consent. Withdrawal ends the active session, expires its searches, and prevents additional downloads for that session.

## If no photos appear

Try a different, well-lit portrait selfie without sunglasses, face coverings, or heavy motion blur. A zero-result search does not prove that no event photos contain you. Ask the photographer for help at `[photographer support contact]` or request an authorized manual review.

Do not use another person's selfie without their permission. The service is designed for event-scoped retrieval, not identifying people across events.
